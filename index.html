Frequently asked questions

CADI learns your business in minutes – transforming company materials into adaptive, conversational AI training systems built to scale seamlessly.

What is CADI?
CADI is an AI-powered performance platform that helps enterprises develop their people the same way they develop their systems — with precision, consistency, and measurable results.
How is CADI different from traditional training systems?
Traditional systems deliver content and track completion. CADI teaches, questions, and coaches in live two-way conversation, grounded in your own material, so learners have to explain, defend, and apply what they know.
How does CADI improve learning and performance?
Every learner moves through the same loop: Teach, Validate, Practice, Improve. CADI builds knowledge, probes understanding with spoken assessment, puts it to work in realistic simulations, and keeps coaching after the session ends.
What business impact does CADI deliver?
Faster onboarding, fewer escalations handled incorrectly, verified readiness before employees face customers, and visibility into exactly where each team needs more training.
Who is CADI designed for?
Enterprise L&D, sales, customer service, compliance and leadership teams; workforce development programs; and high school, college and graduate educators who want proof of understanding, not just completion.
